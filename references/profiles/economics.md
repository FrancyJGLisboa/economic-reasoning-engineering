# Economics profile

Use this profile for prices, markets, incentives, margins, allocation, substitution, and policy effects.
Use it as the default when the requested purpose remains ambiguous.

## Episode selection

Identify the documented economic question, claimed mechanism, necessary conditions, and competing explanations.
Preserve contract, geography, date, units, and data vintage when available.
Separate physical quantities, financial exposure, and accounting measures.
Treat operational constraints as economic mechanisms only when the source supports that connection.

## Extraction attention points

Extract inputs, operations, outputs, alternatives, tests, and abstention conditions only when documented.
Specify the direction and scope of each supported relationship.
Separate spot prices from effective prices after hedges, contracts, taxes, freight, and timing.
Separate scenarios and forecasts from observations.
Do not treat a correlation or expert statement as verified causality.

## Expected outputs and limits

Describe each expected output in the method's `expected_outputs` list.
Documented examples may include a conditional margin comparison, a stock-flow reconciliation, or a bounded demand scenario.
State units, horizon, and interpretation when relevant.
Do not invent numerical inputs or recommend a transaction from incomplete evidence.
