# Operations profile

Use this profile for process execution, delivery feasibility, capacity, dependencies, and failure controls.
Keep the shared evidence workflow and portable package contract.
Do not require an economic claim before accepting an operational episode.

## Episode selection

Identify the process objective, current state, completion condition, and required deadline.
Extract stages, dependencies, queues, resources, capacities, handoffs, and known failure modes when supported.
Separate a source's example from an observed shipment or measured service level.
Retain financial constraints when they affect process execution. Do not infer insolvency from a margin scenario.

## Extraction attention points

1. Extract how the speaker locates an item within the process.
2. Preserve stated sequential dependencies and demonstrated parallel activities.
3. Record quantities, capacity units, calendars, queues, and calculations exactly as supplied.
4. Extract any comparison with deadlines, alternatives, or inventory positions.
5. Preserve documented control points and failure criteria. Mark absent details as NOT_DOCUMENTED.

Distinguish processing time, waiting time, transit time, and buffer time.
A charter tender and production planning may overlap. Do not assume overlap without evidence.
Do not count a buffer twice or treat a best-case estimate as a delivery guarantee.
Preserve source arithmetic. Put requested arithmetic corrections in the optional critique layer.
Do not manufacture a consistent timeline when the source changes dates, quantities, or assumptions.

## Expected outputs

Write named results in `expected_outputs`, with units and interpretation when relevant.
Documented results may include a remaining-stage map, a conditional delivery range, a capacity test, or a delivery-evidence checklist.
Do not add dependencies, controls, or calculations absent from the source.
Unknown durations remain unknown. A useful qualitative result can still identify the evidence required for a decision.

## Fidelity checks and limits

Check that source dependencies, quantities, uncertainty, and conditions survive extraction.
Record missing dates, capacities, and resources as documentation gaps.
Do not invent a bottleneck test or delivery rule the speaker never used.
The profile is an extraction aid. It does not supply the speaker's method.
The fidelity contract controls optional critique and evaluation.
