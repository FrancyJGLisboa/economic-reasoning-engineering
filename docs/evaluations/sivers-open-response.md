# Open-response decision reconstruction: source-recognition audit

## Question and design

Can a faithful context package help reproduce a real person's decision when the question supplies no answer options?
The candidate is Derek Sivers's account of refunding fees and making a digital distribution service free.
The primary source is the [2005 Venture Voice interview](https://sive.rs/2005-11-vv).
The author publishes reuse permission in his [FAQ](https://sive.rs/faq).

The entire target episode and adjacent integration discussion were excluded from the evaluated package.
The retained corpus contains 47 other interview paragraphs with declared identity substitutions.
Extraction produced five episodes, five opinion records, and three documented method fragments.
The portable package has 23 indexed records. It passed validation and relocation checks.

The same open question runs twice per condition, in baseline, package, package, baseline order.
The baseline receives case facts only. The package condition also receives the complete folder rendered as text.
The question asks for action on collected payments, future pricing, decision criteria, evidence, and uncertainty.
There are no candidate actions to select.

The case, reference episode, rubric, package payload, and response schema are frozen before model responses.
The rubric distinguishes the refund decision, ongoing pricing policy, and four explanatory criteria.
Two separate model assessor sessions inspect anonymous responses in reversed order.
The assessor protocol is frozen before inspecting the answer content.
Human review remains separate.

## Source and evaluation limits

The source is a retrospective interview account, not an independently authenticated transaction record.
The investigator already knows the reference decision.
This is an episode split within one interview, not a prospective historical prediction.
Name substitution does not remove recognizable quantities or narrative details.
Two repeated responses do not create two independent historical decisions.
Inline presentation does not evaluate selective reading or filesystem navigation.
The pilot does not separate the effect of source content from the effect of package structure.

## Results

Four answer sessions and two assessor sessions completed on 2026-11-13.
All evaluated responses identified Sivers and CD Baby despite the substituted names.
This recognition also occurred in both baseline sessions.
The selected public case therefore does not isolate new personal-judgment transfer from prior story knowledge.

| Condition | Collected payments | Predicted future policy |
|---|---|---|
| No package, both repetitions | Full refund | Charge after the integration becomes available |
| With package, both repetitions | Full refund | Remove the additional fee |
| Reserved source account | Full refund | Free service as described in that account |

The action prediction differed consistently between conditions and matched the reserved account more closely with the package.
This is a descriptive observation from one case. It does not establish general behavioral equivalence.
Participants stated that they did not rely on remembered outcomes. That declaration cannot verify absence of prior-knowledge influence.
The source account does not establish that every possible future distribution charge was permanently eliminated.

## Assessor results

Two separate assessor sessions evaluated anonymous responses in opposite orders.
Both passes assigned identical dimension scores.
Action agreement received 2/4 without the package and 4/4 with the package.
Explanatory agreement remained 4/8 in both conditions.

Package responses referred more specifically to the subject's musician-oriented priorities.
Baseline responses more explicitly connected external dependency with charging after availability.
Neither condition fully recovered the source explanation about expectations, fixed costs, and scale.
These scores are model assessments. They are not human judgments or independent experimental replications.
Response language may reveal the condition despite anonymous labels.

## Integrity and decision

The frozen source selection, reference episode, rubric, and context payload remained unchanged.
Six cited method references resolved to exact retained anchors.
Response files and assessor files matched the final messages in their runtime logs.
There were no tool actions in evaluated turns.
The runtime ignored malformed local role definitions before the turns. Collaboration was disabled.

The local audit passed valid data and rejected altered frozen inputs and altered responses.
The portable package passed validation and relocation checks.
These checks do not establish semantic fidelity, memory exclusion, or causal truth.

Keep this result as evidence of a context-associated change in one open response.
Do not promote it to proof that a portable folder transfers identical personal judgment.
Do not change the skill's extraction rules from this case alone.
The next confirmatory case should be less recognizable and include human assessment of the reasoning criteria.
The prepared human-review form remains NOT_REVIEWED.

Private source snapshots, responses, model assessments, and review artifacts remain outside this repository.
The installed extraction behavior remains unchanged.
